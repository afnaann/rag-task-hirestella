# Alex Chen — Professional Profile

## Summary

Alex Chen is a software engineer with seven years of industry experience, specialising in machine learning infrastructure, distributed systems, and applied AI. Alex has a track record of building production ML pipelines at scale, contributing to open-source tooling, and leading small cross-functional teams.

Alex holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Edinburgh (2015) and a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from University College London (2017). Both degrees were completed with distinction.

Alex is based in London, United Kingdom, and is currently open to senior engineering and staff-level roles at technology companies working on AI infrastructure, developer tooling, or applied ML products.
